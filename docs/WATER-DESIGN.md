# Water design and continuation notes

The selected direction combines [concept 3](concepts/option-3.png) for calm, barely moving edge refraction and [concept 2](concepts/option-2.png) for localized touch ripples. [Concept 1](concepts/option-1.png) is retained as an earlier alternative. These are generated design references, not captures of the implemented interface.

## Current direction

- Warm ivory circular surface with restrained gold accents.
- Refraction distributed around the perimeter, fading progressively toward a quiet center.
- Slow idle movement; a touch creates a short train of slightly stretched, uneven rings with varied highlights.
- Logo, captions, icons, image previews, inner ring, and edge lighting remain sharp above the water. Do not distort them.
- D toggles the liquid layer without interrupting the scripted demo. Touch orientation and all existing keyboard controls remain available.
- Preserve the thin faded inner ring. Avoid large moving gloss ellipses or mechanically perfect concentric targets.

## Implementation

`assets/water.js` renders a transparent WebGL layer using crossing-wave curvature, perimeter fronts, and up to four short touch disturbances. The backing canvas is capped at 512 pixels and animation is throttled to a maximum of approximately 30 frames per second. Rendering pauses when hidden, disabled, or reduced motion is requested. These are workload limits, not a measured native kiosk frame-rate guarantee.

`assets/water-still.png` is the no-WebGL fallback, generated by `tools/render-water-still.py`. Run the generator after changing the idle surface model so the fallback stays consistent. Install NumPy and Pillow in a development environment if needed; the kiosk does not require them.
